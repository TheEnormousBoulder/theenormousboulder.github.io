PA planner privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026.

PA planner is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the Google scope calendar.app.created, it creates one calendar named "PA plan" and adds, updates and deletes events on that calendar only. It cannot read or change any other calendar, and it does not use any other Google data.

Where data goes

The sign-in token and the plans stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the "PA plan" calendar can be deleted in Google Calendar.
